# BeeProof — AI Service (Phase 5 Architecture Scope)

This service will house the machine learning and predictive intelligence capabilities scheduled for **Phase 5**.

## Phase 5 Scope
- **Hive Health Predictions**: Assessing colony stability, queen health, and swarming propensity based on historical acoustics and climate readings.
- **Disease & Pest Detection**: Early detection models for Varroa destructor mite infestation and American/European foulbrood.
- **Productivity & Yield Forecasting**: Estimating surplus harvest yields by floral blooming windows and cluster telemetry.

## Foundation Entities (Established in Phase 1 Backend)
- `hive_health_predictions`
- `disease_predictions`
- `productivity_predictions`

*Note: Phase 1 establishes the database schema, data models, and persistence contracts. Live predictive pipelines and Python ML engines will be integrated in Phase 5.*
